# NOTICE

Simple RSVP Reader is an independent open-source port and adaptation inspired by and derived in part from RSVP Nano. It is not an official RSVP Nano release.

Original project: https://github.com/ionutdecebal/rsvpnano
RSVP Nano is distributed under the MIT License. Its original copyright and license notice are retained.

This 3.49B V2 port adds board-specific ESP-IDF integration for the Waveshare ESP32-S3 Touch-LCD-3.49B V2 / Rev 1.1, including AXS15231B QSPI display handling, touch I2C handling, SDMMC, TCA9554 system control, RTC/battery integration, a 640×172 landscape UI, and PWR standby/power-off behavior.

The project is independent from Waveshare and from the upstream RSVP Nano project.
